## What Good American's return policy costs you (official numbers)

Return fees are where most discount guides get sloppy — third-party pages cite everything from 14 to 30 days and restocking fees from $2.99 to $9. Good American's own help center settles it:

- **14 days** from the date your order shipped to request a return or exchange.
- **Exchanges and returns for store credit are free.**
- Returns to your original payment method cost **$6.00 per order** at a Happy Returns Return Bar, or **$9.00 per order** with a UPS prepaid mail label.
- Refunds take up to **7–10 business days** to appear after processing.
- Returns started but not dropped off or shipped within 30 days expire automatically.
- Items must be unworn with tags attached. Bodysuits and swimwear have a protective adhesive strip — remove it and the return is rejected.
- Final Sale items can't be returned or exchanged, and retail-store purchases can't be returned to the website.

The free store credit option is quietly the smart move if you shop this brand regularly: it turns a $6–9 fee into zero, though obviously it locks the money into Good American rather than your bank account.

International orders get a harsher deal: a $15 restocking fee per order, refunds only (no exchanges — you return and reorder), and no refund of original taxes, duties, or shipping.
